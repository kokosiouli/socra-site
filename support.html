Common questions

How do I use AIVA? Open Settings in Socra and add your Google Gemini key. AIVA then reads meal photos, estimates calories and discusses your books and stories.

Where is my data? On your iPhone and in your own iCloud. See the privacy policy.

Health readings show a dash. Allow Socra in the Health app under Sharing › Apps, and make sure your watch has recorded that reading.
