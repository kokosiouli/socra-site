Privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026. Socra is made by Panajotis Kokosiouli.

The short version

What you log in Socra stays with you: on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. We run no servers that hold your data, we show no ads, we do not track you across apps or websites, and we never sell or share your data.

What Socra keeps

  • Your entries: feelings, meals, drinks, weight, notes, gratitude, rides and favourites. They are stored on your device and synced to your private iCloud account through Apple's CloudKit, so only you can see them.
  • Rides record speed, distance, route and motion on your device while a ride is running.

Apple Health

With your permission Socra reads Health data such as heart rate, sleep, steps, calories burned and other readings from your watch, to show them to you. Health data is never used for advertising, never shared with third parties and never leaves your device except where you choose to ask AIVA about it. You can change Socra's access at any time in the Health app under Sharing › Apps.

Services Socra talks to

  • AIVA (Google Gemini): only if you add your own Gemini key. When you ask AIVA to read a meal or to discuss something, the photo or words you send go to Google to get the answer. Your key is kept in your iPhone's Keychain.
  • Weather (Open-Meteo): your approximate location is sent to get the local weather. It is not stored.
  • Apple Music: with your permission, Socra plays your Apple Music library through Apple.
  • Markets (Binance public prices): prices are fetched from Binance's public service. If you add a read-only key, it stays in your Keychain; Socra never trades or moves money.
  • Stories and news: public feeds are fetched to show you readings and headlines; nothing about you is sent.

Camera, photos, location and motion

The camera and photos are used only when you choose to photograph a meal. Location and motion are used only for rides and the weather.

Children

Socra is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can delete any entry in the app, remove all data by deleting the app and its iCloud data (Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage), and turn off any permission in Settings.
